About TuneIn

TuneIn is a modern, fully-featured web-based music player. It features a dynamic UI, responsive design, dark/light themes, intuitive controls, keyboard shortcuts, and rich playback functionality. Designed for style and simplicity, TuneIn makes listening seamless and enjoyable across devices.

Dark/Light Mode

Personalize your experience with theme toggling based on preference or time of day.

Shuffle Playback

Enjoy randomness and spontaneity with one-click shuffle for your playlist.

Responsive & Accessible

Keyboard-friendly and mobile-optimized to work beautifully on all devices.

Volume & Mute

Fine-tune your listening volume or mute instantly with elegant controls.
